CMT Finesse U-20

CMT Finesse U-20 is a grassroots football tournament for young players under 20. I founded it and I organise it, from planning and logistics to partnerships and match days.

4Editions so far
HundredsPlayers and spectators
BrandPartnerships secured

What Is CMT?

The tournament gives young footballers in the community a proper stage to play on. It has grown with every edition, and the most recent one, the 4th edition, ran over two days in August, drawing hundreds of players and spectators together.

What We Aim to Achieve

  • Give young talented players a platform to be seen and to compete
  • Bring the community together around football
  • Build partnerships with brands that support youth sport
  • Grow bigger and better with every edition

My Role

As founder and organiser, I lead the planning, coordinate the people involved and work with partners to make each edition happen. Running it has taught me leadership, teamwork, and how to manage a project from idea to match day.
